# Introduction-to-PyPSA-Course

💡 Looking to master grid modeling and power system optimization? ⚡🇵🇭

I am sharing the recorded video of my workshop on Introduction to PyPSA (Python for Power System Analysis) in Filipino. This training was previously conducted at UP Diliman through the IIEE-CSC-UPD , as well as for the Ateneo Electronics and Computer Engineering Society and the University of Santo Tomas (UST) in coordination with their faculties.

Whether you are a Filipino engineering student or a practicing engineer, this introduction to PyPSA will help you dive into open-source energy system modeling and renewable integration.
